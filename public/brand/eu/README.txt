Official "Funded by the European Union" emblem (horizontal), as supplied by
the European Commission in EPS format (print file by DG COMM, 2021) and
converted to SVG. Shapes and layout are untouched. The print file is in
CMYK, so its two colours were set to the EU's official screen values:
EU blue #003399 (RGB 0/51/153) and EU yellow #FFCC00 (RGB 255/204/0).
If the official RGB (screen) version of the file is downloaded later,
it can replace these files directly.

  EN-Funded_by_the_EU-POS.svg  colour version for white or light backgrounds (used on the site)
  EN-Funded_by_the_EU-NEG.svg  negative version for dark backgrounds

Rules (DG ECHO and EU emblem guidelines):
- Never redraw, recolour, stretch, crop or add effects to these files.
- The EU flag must be at least 1 cm high (about 38 px) and at least as big as
  the biggest other logo shown with it (here, the NRC logo).
- Keep clear space around the emblem; do not put lines or other logos next to it.
- The emblem links to DG ECHO's website, as required for partner websites:
  https://civil-protection-humanitarian-aid.ec.europa.eu/
- To add Spanish, French or other EU languages, download the official files
  from the DG ECHO partner helpdesk (Visibility > Visual identity), place them
  here and list them in src/components/EuEmblem.jsx (OFFICIAL map).
- Do not add the "Funded by European Union Humanitarian Aid" field logo to
  this website. That version is for field items (signboards, relief items,
  staff clothing). Websites use "Funded by the European Union" only.

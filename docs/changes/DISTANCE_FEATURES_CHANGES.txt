Distance and supply link features, October 2026
===============================================

This version contains everything in ECHO_VISIBILITY_CHANGES.txt (keep all
of that exactly as it is), plus these new features. All unit tests (12) and
browser tests (13) pass, and dist has been rebuilt.

1. Supply lines now measure distance, like Google Maps' measure tool
   - White dot at each click, dashed line following the mouse, running km
     label next to the mouse.
   - Points snap to icons (highlight ring shows the icon code).
   - Start on a source market, click along the road, click the marketplace:
     the line finishes by itself, the two markets are linked and the
     distance is filled in.
   - Also finishes by clicking the last point again, double-clicking or
     pressing Enter. Backspace or "Undo point" removes the last point.
   - Click a finished line to see its link and length; drag its white dots
     to adjust the route.
   New file: src/components/DrawingLayer.jsx, src/geo.js
   Changed: src/components/MapCanvas.jsx, src/components/StatusBanner.jsx,
   src/store.js

2. Many source markets to many marketplaces
   - Marketplace form: "Supplied by" checklist of source markets, each with
     a distance field (filled from the supply line if one was drawn).
   - Source market form: "Supplies" checklist of marketplaces.
   - Distance box lists every source market to marketplace pair
     (e.g. "SM1 -> MP1  45.2 km"), grouped by marketplace, plus any source
     market not yet linked.
   Changed: src/components/MarkerEditModal.jsx, src/components/DistanceList.jsx,
   src/store.js

3. Measure tool (new "Measure" button in the toolbar)
   - Measures any path. If it starts or ends on a marketplace, the result
     can be saved as that marketplace's distance from the target population,
     or as a source market to marketplace distance.
   - The measuring line is never printed.
   Changed: src/components/Toolbar.jsx, src/components/StatusBanner.jsx

4. Legend and distance box never hide rows
   - They grow to the full map height, then continue in a second column.
     Before, they stopped at 45% of the map height and rows were cut off
     in the downloaded PNG.
   New file: src/useOverlayColumns.js
   Changed: src/components/Legend.jsx, src/components/DistanceList.jsx,
   src/styles/app.css, src/export.js

5. Text and help
   - All new text in English, Spanish, French and Arabic
     (src/i18n/translations.js). Arabic shows mixed text such as
     "62 km, gravel road" the right way round.
   - Help guide: updated "Draw a supply line" and "Edit market details",
     new section "Measure a distance" (now 9 sections; test updated).
   - README.md: new section "Distances, supply links and the Measure tool".
   - New test: tests/links.test.mjs.
